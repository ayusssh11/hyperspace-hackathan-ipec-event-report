# 🚀 Hyperspace Innovation Hackathon 2026

![Hyperspace Hackathon Banner](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=view&id=19UVVuI7kZvJqCt_0W5uLRC0046ayelO_)

## 🌟 About the Event
**India’s Biggest, Boldest, and Most Visionary Deep-Tech Hackathon.**

Hosted at **IPEC Ghaziabad** and powered by **Hack2Skill**, **Hyperspace 2026** was not just another college event—it was a revolution. Over an electrifying **36-hour marathon**, participants dived into the most advanced technologies transforming our world.

With ecosystem support from global tech giants **Microsoft** and **AWS**, and real-world problem statements inspired by **ISRO & DRDO**, this event stood at the forefront of India’s innovation journey.

> *"This is where imagination meets engineering, and engineering meets impact."*
